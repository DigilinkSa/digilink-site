Decisions we support

Validate the case before committing capital.

A useful feasibility study makes assumptions visible, tests uncertainty and gives leadership a defensible basis for investment.

01 / NEW CAPACITY

New factory or production line

Evaluate market need, capacity, process flow, equipment, location considerations, cost and financial return.

02 / MODERNIZATION

Upgrade or automate existing operations

Test whether proposed equipment, digitalization or automation addresses the actual performance constraint.

03 / TRANSFORMATION

Prioritize competing initiatives

Compare business cases so investment follows value, readiness, risk and strategic fit.

Study components

A decision-ready view of the opportunity.

01

Market & demand

Demand assumptions, customer segments, competition and sales scenarios.

02

Technical concept

Products, process, capacity, equipment, utilities, layout and staffing assumptions.

03

CAPEX & OPEX

Capital requirements, operating-cost structure and working-capital assumptions.

04

Financial model

Revenue, cash flow, profitability, return metrics and funding scenarios.

05

Risk & sensitivity

Critical assumptions, downside scenarios, dependencies and mitigations.

06

Implementation roadmap

Decision gates, schedule, procurement, commissioning and ramp-up milestones.
