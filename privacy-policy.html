Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026

DigiLink respects the privacy of clients, prospects, partners and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal data when you interact with DigiLink through our website, business communications, customer relationship management systems, Microsoft 365 services, or the WhatsApp Business Platform.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact information, such as your name, company, job title, email address and telephone or WhatsApp number.
  • Business information you provide in connection with enquiries, proposals, assessments, projects or support requests.
  • Communication content and related metadata from email, WhatsApp, meetings, calls and other business channels.
  • Technical information needed to operate our website and digital services, such as basic logs, timestamps and security records.

2. How we use personal data

We may use personal data to:

  • Respond to enquiries and communicate with clients, prospects and partners.
  • Prepare proposals, quotations, assessments, project deliverables and related professional services.
  • Maintain CRM records and a business communication history.
  • Send and receive business messages through approved communication channels, including WhatsApp Business.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve our website, ERP and related systems.
  • Meet legal, contractual, audit, security and record-keeping obligations where applicable.

3. WhatsApp Business communications

When you communicate with DigiLink through WhatsApp, your telephone number, profile information made available by WhatsApp, message content, delivery status and related metadata may be processed so that DigiLink can receive, respond to and maintain an appropriate business communication record.

WhatsApp services are provided by Meta and are also subject to Meta's and WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices.

4. Service providers and data sharing

DigiLink does not sell personal data. We may use trusted technology and hosting providers to operate our services, including providers of cloud hosting, databases, business email, authentication and business messaging. Personal data is shared only to the extent reasonably necessary to provide, secure or support those services, or where required by law.

5. Data retention

We retain personal and business information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including ongoing client relationships, project records, contractual requirements, audit needs, security, dispute resolution and applicable legal obligations.

6. Security

DigiLink applies reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Access to business systems is limited according to role and operational need.

7. Your choices and rights

You may contact DigiLink to ask about personal data we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion where applicable and legally permitted. We will handle such requests in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

8. International and third-party processing

Some technology providers used by DigiLink may process data using infrastructure located outside Saudi Arabia. Where applicable, DigiLink seeks to use reputable providers and appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable requirements.

9. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, legal obligations or technology arrangements change. The effective date shown above indicates the latest revision.

10. Contact DigiLink

For privacy questions or requests, contact DigiLink at info@digilink.sa or visit www.digilink.sa.

DigiLink is based in Industrial Jubail, Saudi Arabia.
