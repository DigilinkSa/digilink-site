Discrete manufacturing engagement

Saudi Wood Products Manufacturer

Building the operating foundation for connected, measurable production before committing to advanced automation and AI.

Anonymized engagement · Representative visual
SectorWood-products manufacturing
EngagementOfficial SIRI assessment + implementation program
Primary focusData, ERP, connectivity and targeted pilots

The challenge

Build the basics before buying advanced technology.

The factory was strengthening its production and inventory systems while shop-floor reporting, machine connectivity, operational intelligence and formal workforce development remained limited. The transformation needed to be gradual, measurable and economically justified.

01

Siloed production information

Order, planning, production, quality, inventory and dispatch handoffs required clearer processes and shared master data.

02

Limited equipment connectivity

Priority woodworking machines and facility assets did not provide a consistent digital view of run, stop, fault or output data.

03

Insufficient loss visibility

Downtime, scrap, rework, quality defects and energy losses needed agreed definitions, capture routines and dashboards.

04

Technology choices needed proof

AI visual inspection, condition monitoring and automation opportunities required pilots and business cases before wider investment.

DigiLink engagement

A twelve-month path from discipline to intelligence.

The program prioritized operating foundations first, then introduced connectivity, measurement, improvement and advanced technology only when evidence supported investment.

01 / ORGANIZE

Processes and data

Standardize product, material, routing, asset, quality and responsibility data.

02 / DIGITIZE

Use the factory system

Strengthen production, inventory, quality, maintenance and work-order discipline.

03 / CONNECT

Pilot priority assets

Connect two or three important machines and establish secure operational data capture.

04 / IMPROVE

Act on measured losses

Use performance, quality, maintenance and energy evidence to select improvement projects and pilots.

Verified engagement outcomes

A clear management program for the next transformation stage.

Official maturity baseline

The SIRI assessment established a structured view of current readiness and the most important foundation gaps.

Twelve-month program

A practical implementation and governance program sequenced data, systems, connectivity, measurement and improvement.

Investment decision framework

Technology pilots—including AI visual quality inspection—were tied to defined problems, baselines and acceptance criteria.

Roadmap targets for data adoption, downtime, quality, inventory, maintenance and energy remain implementation measures. The reviewed evidence confirms the assessment and program design, not the realization of those benefits.
